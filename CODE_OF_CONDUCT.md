# Code of Conduct

Chiasm should be a project where people can ask questions, contribute small pieces, disagree about design and learn in public without being treated dismissively.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- assume good intent while still correcting inaccurate claims;
- make room for beginners and people working in a second language;
- respect different experience levels, platforms and development styles;
- disclose relevant conflicts or source/publisher relationships when they affect a technical or registry discussion;
- protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The project does not accept:

- harassment, threats or discriminatory abuse;
- sexualized or demeaning comments directed at participants;
- deliberate intimidation, stalking or doxxing;
- publishing another person's private information;
- repeated bad-faith disruption after a maintainer has asked for it to stop;
- using project spaces to pressure people into political, commercial or personal positions unrelated to the work.

Technical disagreement, criticism of code, and asking for evidence are not misconduct when expressed professionally.

## Where this applies

This applies to Melodex-controlled project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, review threads and project-managed community channels.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, restrict participation, reject contributions or block accounts when necessary to keep project spaces usable and safe.

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behaviour and should distinguish an ordinary mistake from repeated or deliberate misconduct.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Use GitHub's built-in report/block tools for platform abuse. If a maintainer has published a private contact method on their GitHub profile, that may also be used for a project-specific conduct concern.

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct process.

## Project values

The project prefers:

```text
evidence over status
specific feedback over ridicule
truthful claims over marketing
small useful contributions over gatekeeping
```

This code of conduct governs participation in the Melodex project; it does not change the licence of contributed code.
